Fuel consumption is one of the most confusing areas of unit conversion because the two main systems don't just use different units—they use different **logic**. One measures efficiency (how far can I go?), while the other measures consumption (how much fuel do I need?). This guide provides the tools to bridge the gap between **Liters per 100km (L/100km)** and **Miles Per Gallon (MPG)**.

### 1. The Inverse Relationship

The most important thing to remember is that these two units are "upside down" relative to each other.
*   **MPG:** Distance / Fuel
*   **L/100km:** Fuel / Distance

Because of this, you can't just multiply by a constant factor like 1.6 or 2.2. You have to perform a division.

### 2. The US MPG Calculation

To convert between L/100km and **US MPG**, the magic number is **235.21**.

*   **Formula:** `235.21 / (L/100km) = US MPG`
*   **Formula:** `235.21 / (US MPG) = L/100km`

**Example:** A fuel-efficient car uses **6.0 L/100km**.
`235.21 / 6.0 = 39.2 MPG`.

### 3. The UK (Imperial) MPG Calculation

As we noted in our fuel economy guide, the UK uses a larger gallon. The magic number for **Imperial MPG** is **282.48**.

*   **Formula:** `282.48 / (L/100km) = UK MPG`

**Example:** That same **6.0 L/100km** car in London would be advertised as having **47.1 MPG**. 

### 4. Why Use L/100km? (The Consumption Advantage)

While MPG is more intuitive for seeing "how far" a tank will take you, L/100km is better for calculating your **wallet impact**.
If you drive 20,000 km per year:
*   At **10 L/100km**, you will use 2,000 liters of gas.
*   At **8 L/100km**, you will use 1,600 liters of gas.
The saving (400 liters) is immediate and clear. In the MPG system, the savings are much harder to calculate mentally.

### 5. Quick Reference "Anchor Points"

Memorizing a few common pairs can help you estimate without a calculator:
*   **5 L/100km** ≈ 47 MPG (Hybrid)
*   **8 L/100km** ≈ 29 MPG (Mid-size car)
*   **10 L/100km** ≈ 23.5 MPG (SUV)
*   **15 L/100km** ≈ 15.5 MPG (Truck/Performance car)

### Conclusion

Whether you're shopping for a car in a different country or just trying to understand your trip computer while on vacation, the L/100km to MPG conversion is a vital skill. Remember that they are inverses, keep the number **235** in mind, and you'll never be stuck at the pump doing complex math again.
