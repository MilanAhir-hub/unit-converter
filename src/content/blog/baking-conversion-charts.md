In the world of cooking, a little extra salt might just make a dish more savory. But in the world of **baking**, a little extra flour can turn a light sponge cake into a dense brick. Baking is a series of chemical reactions, and like any science experiment, it requires precise measurement. This guide provides the essential charts to help you convert any recipe into the golden standard of baking: weight.

### 1. The Flour Chart

Flour is the most difficult ingredient to measure by volume. Depending on how much it has "settled" in the bag, a cup of flour can weigh anywhere from 120g to 160g.

| Volume | All-Purpose | Bread Flour | Cake Flour |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1/4 Cup** | 31g | 32g | 28g |
| **1/3 Cup** | 42g | 43g | 38g |
| **1/2 Cup** | 63g | 64g | 56g |
| **1 Cup** | 125g | 127g | 114g |

### 2. The Sugar and Sweetener Chart

Sugar is generally more consistent than flour, but it still varies by type.

*   **Granulated (White) Sugar:** 1 cup = 200g
*   **Brown Sugar (Packed):** 1 cup = 220g
*   **Powdered (Icing) Sugar:** 1 cup = 120g
*   **Honey / Maple Syrup:** 1 cup = 340g

### 3. Fats and Dairy

Measuring butter by the "tablespoon" can be messy. Using a scale is much cleaner.

*   **Butter (1 stick):** 113g (or 8 tablespoons)
*   **Vegetable Oil:** 1 cup = 218g
*   **Milk / Heavy Cream:** 1 cup = 240g (very close to water)
*   **Yogurt / Sour Cream:** 1 cup = 245g

### 4. Small Measurements: The Precision Rule

For ingredients like baking soda, salt, and spices, even a small error can ruin a dish. While weight is best, high-quality measuring spoons are usually sufficient for these tiny amounts.
*   **1 tsp Salt:** 6g
*   **1 tsp Baking Soda:** 6g
*   **1 tsp Baking Powder:** 5g
*   **1 tsp Yeast (Dry):** 3g

### 5. Why You Should Use a Digital Scale

If you are serious about baking, a $20 digital scale is the best investment you can make. 
1.  **Consistency:** Your favorite cookies will taste exactly the same every time.
2.  **Less Cleanup:** You can pour ingredients directly into the bowl (using the "Tare" or "Zero" button) instead of washing five different measuring cups.
3.  **International Recipes:** You can finally tackle that French macaron recipe without having to guess what "150g of egg whites" looks like.

### Conclusion

Precision is the "secret ingredient" in every successful bakery. By moving away from the guesswork of cups and adopting the accuracy of grams, you'll find that your baking becomes more reliable, more professional, and ultimately, more delicious.
